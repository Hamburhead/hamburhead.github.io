Pigeon GO! Privacy Policy

Last updated: 5 October 2026

Pigeon GO! (“the game”) is a mobile game for Android made by Sologames, an independent developer (“we”, “us”). This policy explains what information is used when you play, why, who receives it and the choices you have.

Questions or requests: hamburhead.anim@gmail.com

In short
  • There is no account. We never ask for your name, email address or other contact details.
  • Your progress is saved on your device.
  • We use Google Firebase for usage statistics and crash reports. You can turn usage statistics off in Settings.
  • The game shows ads through Unity LevelPlay. Ad partners use your device’s advertising ID. In the EEA, the UK and Switzerland we ask for your consent first, and you can change your choice at any time.

1. Information saved on your device

Your coins, upgrades, skins, missions, daily gift, settings and best score are saved only on your device. We do not receive them. Uninstalling the game deletes them.

2. Usage statistics (Google Firebase Analytics)

What: game events such as starting and finishing a run, score, coins earned and spent, store screens opened, ads watched and tutorial steps; information about the device and the app (device model, Android version, language, app version); an app-instance identifier created by Firebase; and your approximate location (country or region, derived from your IP address). In this game Firebase Analytics does not collect your advertising ID.

Why: to understand how the game is played and to improve its balance, difficulty and stability.

Your choice: turn off Settings › Share usage data in the game and no more usage statistics are sent.

3. Crash reports (Google Firebase Crashlytics)

When the game crashes or meets an error, a technical report is sent: what went wrong in the code, device model, Android version, app version, a Crashlytics installation identifier and the time. We use it only to find and fix bugs.

4. Advertising

The game shows rewarded ads, which you choose to watch in exchange for a reward, and interstitial ads between runs. Remove Ads and VIP turn interstitial ads off; rewarded ads stay available because you choose them.

Ads are delivered through Unity LevelPlay (Unity Technologies and its affiliate ironSource Mobile), a mediation service that picks which ad network shows each ad. The networks used today are ironSource Ads and Unity Ads. If we add networks, we will update this list.

To show ads, these partners may collect: your device’s advertising ID, IP address, device information (model, Android version, language, connection type), information about the app, and how you interact with ads (views, clicks, whether an ad was completed). They use it to deliver and measure ads, limit how often you see them, prevent fraud and, where you have agreed, show ads based on your interests.

For each ad shown, the game records in Firebase Analytics which network showed it, its format and the revenue it generated (“ad_impression”).

5. Consent and privacy choices

  • EEA, United Kingdom and Switzerland: the first time you open the game, a consent form from Google’s User Messaging Platform (based on the IAB Transparency & Consent Framework) asks whether our ad partners may use your data for personalised ads and related purposes. You can accept, reject or choose purpose by purpose. If you do not consent, you may still see ads, but they are not personalised. You can change your choice at any time in Settings › Privacy choices.
  • Everywhere: you can reset or delete your advertising ID in your Android settings (Settings › Google › Ads). Deleting it stops apps, including this game, from using it for personalised ads.
  • Usage statistics: Settings › Share usage data, as described above.

6. In-app purchases

Purchases are processed by Google Play. We never receive your card or payment details; we only receive confirmation of what was bought so that the game can deliver it.

7. Children

The game is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from them. If you believe a child has provided information through the game, contact us and we will delete what we can.

8. Legal bases (EEA, UK and Switzerland)

  • Usage statistics and crash reports: our legitimate interest in improving the game and fixing errors. You can object at any time with Share usage data or by contacting us.
  • Personalised advertising: your consent, which you can withdraw in Privacy choices.
  • Non-personalised advertising, ad measurement and fraud prevention: our legitimate interest in funding a free game.
  • In-app purchases: performance of the purchase you make.

9. How long information is kept

Information on your device stays until you uninstall the game. Usage statistics, crash reports and advertising data are kept by each provider only as long as needed for the purposes above, according to its own retention settings, and are then deleted or anonymised.

10. Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct or delete your information, to object to or restrict its use, to data portability and to withdraw consent. We do not know who you are, so to act on a request we may need an identifier from your device; write to us and we will help. In the EEA you can also complain to your data protection authority (in Spain, the Agencia Española de Protección de Datos).

11. International transfers

Google, Unity and ironSource may process information outside your country, including in the United States, with the safeguards required by law (such as the EU Standard Contractual Clauses).

12. Partner privacy policies

  • Google (Firebase): policies.google.com/privacy · How Google uses data from apps that use its services
  • Unity (Unity Ads and LevelPlay): Game Player and App User Privacy Policy
  • ironSource (ironSource Ads): ironSource Mobile Privacy Policy

13. Changes

If this policy changes, we will update the date above and, for important changes, let you know in the game.
